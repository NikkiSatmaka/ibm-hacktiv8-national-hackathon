Generative AI can produce new content such as text, images, code, audio, and video.

Large Language Models are examples of generative AI systems that can generate and understand text.

Generative AI can be useful for:

- Brainstorming
- Summarizing information
- Explaining concepts
- Drafting content
- Assisting with programming

However, generative AI also has limitations.
A model may generate information that sounds convincing but is incorrect. This is commonly referred to as hallucination.
Students should therefore verify important information using reliable sources.

For this course, students should follow the CLEAR framework when using Generative AI.

CLEAR means:

- Check
  Check important AI-generated claims against trusted sources.
- Label
  Clearly disclose when AI was substantially used to create academic work.
- Evaluate
  Evaluate whether the AI response is relevant, accurate, and appropriate.
- Avoid Sensitive Data
  Do not provide confidential, personal, or sensitive information to AI systems unless explicitly permitted.
- Remain Responsible
  The student remains responsible for the final submitted work, even when AI tools were used.

Generative AI should support learning rather than replace the student's own understanding.
Students must not submit AI-generated answers without reviewing and understanding them.
